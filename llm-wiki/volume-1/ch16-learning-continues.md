## Content

A short closing chapter encouraging continued learning by studying how real
systems are built.

- **Real-world systems** — study published architectures and design docs for
  large-scale systems to see the building blocks (scaling, replication, caching,
  sharding, queues, consistency) applied in practice.
- **Company engineering blogs** — follow engineering blogs from major companies
  (e.g. large tech and infrastructure teams) for real designs, incident
  write-ups, and evolving best practices.

The transferable lesson across Volume 1: master a small set of building blocks
and reason about their trade-offs for the problem at hand, rather than
memorizing specific architectures.
