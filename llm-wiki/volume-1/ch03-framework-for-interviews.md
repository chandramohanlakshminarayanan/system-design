# A Framework for System Design Interviews

**Summary**: A repeatable 4-step process — understand scope, propose high-level design, deep dive, wrap up — with suggested time allocation.

## Content

A 4-step process for an effective system design interview:

1. **Understand the problem and establish design scope** — ask clarifying
   questions; pin down functional and non-functional requirements, scale, and
   constraints. Do not rush to a solution.
2. **Propose high-level design and get buy-in** — draw boxes and arrows
   (clients, APIs, services, storage), walk through one or two flows, and get
   the interviewer's agreement before going deeper.
3. **Design deep dive** — zoom into the components that matter most (data model,
   a hot path, a tricky consistency problem); discuss alternatives and justify
   choices.
4. **Wrap up** — summarize, discuss bottlenecks, error cases, operations
   (monitoring, rollout), and how you'd scale further.

**Time allocation** (≈45 min): step 1 ~3–10 min, step 2 ~10–15 min, step 3
~10–25 min, step 4 ~3–5 min. The interview evaluates how you think and
communicate, not recall of a canonical answer.
